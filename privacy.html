This notice explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website or write to us about WATAM DeFi Intelligence. It covers this website and email enquiries only. If you run a pilot or use the software, your organisation's agreement and data processing agreement apply to that.

Who is responsible

The controller is WeAreTheArtMakers, Osmanağa Mah. Kırtasiyeci Sok., 34714 Kadıköy – İstanbul, Türkiye. Email: admin@wearetheartmakers.com · Phone: +90 505 314 93 60.

What this website does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking.
  • It has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and no social-media plug-ins.
  • It loads no third-party resources: fonts, images, the video and its captions are served from this site itself.
  • It has no forms. Contact is by email only.

Hosting

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (United States). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the requested page, date and time, and your browser's user agent to deliver the page and keep the service secure. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes (GitHub Pages: data collection). We do not receive these logs. GitHub processes them under its own privacy statement.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in presenting our software securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email enquiries

If you write to us, we process the data you send: typically your name, email address, organisation, role and the content of your message, plus anything you attach.

  • Purpose: answering your enquiry, scoping a possible pilot, and preparing or performing an agreement.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performing that contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For general business communication, our legitimate interest in responding (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Please do not send confidential datasets, credentials, API keys, private keys or passwords by email.
  • Email provider: our mailbox is operated by an email service provider, which processes messages on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for up to two years after our last contact, unless a contract follows. In that case, correspondence is kept as long as the contract requires and for the retention periods set by commercial and tax law.

Who receives it

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is only processed by the providers named above, or disclosed where we are legally required to.

International transfers

GitHub and our email provider may process data outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. Such transfers are subject to the GDPR and to Article 9 of Law No. 6698; you can ask us which safeguard a transfer relies on.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data, and have it rectified or erased;
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR).

To exercise them, write to admin@wearetheartmakers.com or to the postal address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work.

For processing under Turkish law, see our KVKK aydınlatma metni for your rights under Law No. 6698.

No automated decisions

We make no automated decisions or profiles about visitors.

Changes

We will update this notice when our processing changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
